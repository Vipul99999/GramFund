# Operator Onboarding Program

## 7-Day Plan
- Day 1: Trust principles + no-money-holding policy.
- Day 2: Assisted account operations and family communication.
- Day 3: Receipt + evidence workflows.
- Day 4: Daily cash close and variance handling.
- Day 5: Dispute handling and incident protocol.
- Day 6: Fraud red flags and escalation matrix.
- Day 7: Mock drill + certification.

## Certification Requirement
- Minimum 90% checklist compliance in mock operations.
